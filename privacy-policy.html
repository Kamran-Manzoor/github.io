Privacy Policy — Subscription Tracker & Bills

Effective date: 09-08-2026 · Contact: ktsquares@gmail.com

The short version

Your payment records (names, amounts, due dates, notes) are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, and no server of ours that receives your financial data. The app is free and supported by ads, and the advertising and analytics services described below collect limited technical data to function.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter — subscriptions, bills, amounts, due dates, categories, notes, and payment history — is saved in a local database on your device. We cannot see it, access it, or recover it. Uninstalling the app, or using your device's "Clear data" option, permanently deletes it. You can export your data at any time as CSV files from Settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect and process device information such as your advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA/UK), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can choose non-personalized ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings. For details, see how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used (for example, which features are opened and general device/region statistics) so we can improve it. This data is not linked to your name or identity by us — the app never asks who you are.

Notifications

Payment reminders are scheduled and delivered locally on your device. Reminder content never leaves your phone.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your data. We do not share the payment information you enter with anyone. The only data leaving your device is the advertising and analytics data described above, processed by Google as our service provider.

Data deletion

Because your records exist only on your device, deleting the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them permanently. To limit ad-related data, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings. For data held by Google's services, see the Google links above.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
